## WOC Problem Statement & Consulting Mandate
WOC is experiencing severe operational friction due to multi-channel customer support chaos. Inquiries arrive independently across phone calls, emails, web forms, and Instagram DMs without a centralized CRM system. This fragmentation leads to slow response times, inconsistent answers, and scattered customer data. 

As external IS consultants, our mandate is to design an integrated CRM strategy supported by business intelligence dashboards, cloud infrastructure planning, an AI-enabled customer service prototype, and a robust AI governance framework.

Our repository is organized progressively to reflect our semester-long consulting engagement:

* **`/milestone-01/`** — Tableau Data Visualization & Support Insights

  * *Artifacts:* Current-state workflow mapping and CRM evaluation notes.
* **`/milestone-03/`** — AI-Enabled Customer Engagement Prototype  
  * *Artifacts:* Proof-of-concept custom AI assistant/GPT documentation, prompt engineering logs, and interaction test results.
* **`/milestone-04/`** — Cloud Infrastructure Evaluation  
  * *Artifacts:* Google Cloud Compute Engine deployment assessment, cost analysis, and scalability/security review.
* **`/milestone-05/`** — AI Opportunity Evaluation & Business Case  
  * *Artifacts:* Build vs. buy analysis and executive proposal evaluating business value and feasibility.
* **`/milestone-06/`** — Cybersecurity, Privacy, & AI Governance Plan  
  * *Artifacts:* Enterprise risk register, human-oversight escalation protocols, and a phased implementation roadmap (30/60/90-day and 6–12-month rollout).
